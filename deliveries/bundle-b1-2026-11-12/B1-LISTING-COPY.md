AT A GLANCE
One wedding, two tools. This bundle pairs the 34-page printable Wedding Binder with the Wedding Budget Spreadsheet, so you can plan on paper and let the spreadsheet do the sums. Bought separately, the two cost $25.98. This bundle is $19.99.

WHAT'S INSIDE
1. Wedding Binder — 34 pages in 4 versions: English A4, English US Letter, Traditional Chinese A4, Traditional Chinese US Letter. Countdown checklists (12 months out to the wedding day), budget overview and spend log, vendor comparison and payment log, guest list and seating plan, notes pages and a wedding-day timeline. Print the pages you need and file them in your own binder.
2. Wedding Budget Spreadsheet — two Excel files: Blank (fill in your own figures) and Filled Example (a worked example: Emma and Liam, $30,000 budget, wedding 23 October 2027). 16 sheets: Budget Overview with automatic totals for 15 categories, Vendor Payments, Payment Timeline, Task Checklist, Timeline, Guest List, Seating Plan, Vendor Comparison, Catering & Menu, Attire & Accessories, Packing & Day-Of, Shopping List, Stationery & Thank-You, Decor & Inspiration, plus Custom Categories & Notes. Made for Microsoft Excel; also opens in Google Sheets and Apple Numbers.

HOW THE TWO FIT TOGETHER
The binder says it on page 11: copy the figures from the spreadsheet, or fill in by hand. Use the spreadsheet to calculate — totals, paid and pending, what is left — and the binder to write, file and carry the plan. The binder is the paper companion; the spreadsheet does the sums.
