A: Two ways in. Excel: download the .xlsx file and open it — if Excel says "Protected View", click "Enable Editing" and the file is yours. Google Sheets: the Start Here PDF in your download has a ready-made copy link — click it, press "Make a copy", and your own editable copy lands in your Google Drive. The view-only sheet you see first is the shared master; it stays view-only so every buyer starts from the same clean file.

Q: How do I make it mine once I have the copy?
A: Open this month's tab and type in the light-blue cells only: your income, each bill with its due day, what you spend in each category, and what you save. Log spends in the Expense Log as you go, and put each debt on the Debt Payoff page once. The Year at a Glance page fills itself — there is nothing to type there, and nothing else to set up.

Q: Can I use it on my phone?
A: Yes. Your copy opens in the free Google Sheets app, and the .xlsx opens in the free Excel app — you type in the same light-blue cells. Phone screens are smaller, so the layout looks different from a computer, but the sheets and cells are the same. You need a free Google account for the Sheets copy; no subscription and no other software for either route.
