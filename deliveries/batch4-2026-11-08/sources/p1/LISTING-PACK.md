Moving your parent, one calm page at a time.

If you are doing this between work and everything else, you are not behind, and you are not doing it wrong. This Command Center was made for exactly those weeks: start with just three things today, and let the rest wait its turn. Three honest paths are built in - a planned move, a two-week move-in, and "move first, sort the house later." Your family's way is a real way.

Your parent decides. It is their home and their things - one whole page (p.33) is theirs, in their own words. You are there to help it happen, not to take it over.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Pick your path (p.3) - planned, two-week, or move-first-sort-later
2. Measure the new home first (p.6-10) - so nothing you move makes a return trip
3. Sort one room at a time (p.15-21) - six calm ways for every item to go
4. Keep the family on one page (p.22-26) - one claims list, one deadline, one weekly update
5. Run move day from one sheet (p.27-28) - every box numbered, every role named

WHAT'S INSIDE (43 pages)
• Start Here: which path you are on, the first family conversation, the whole move on one page

• Sorting the House: the six ways an item can go (Move / Family / Sell / Donate / Let Go / Decide Later), room-by-room pages, keepsake story cards, decide-later box rules, a donation & sale record

• Quick Start (one-page first week, both fast paths, and a worked example story)
• Command sheets: a one-page move dashboard and a money & paper trail page
• AI photo inventory prompt cards: six cards that turn room photos and spoken notes into draft lists - you stay the editor, and the decider
