Privacy Policy

Savings Printables · Last updated October 17, 2026

What this is

Savings Printables sells printable money trackers and planners. Our internal Pinterest integration ("Savings Printables Uploader") is used solely by us to publish pins for our own products to our own Pinterest account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

Through the Pinterest API, the integration can read our own account, boards, and pins, and create pins on our own boards. It does not access any other Pinterest user's data.

Data we collect from visitors

Our website does not require accounts. If you join our free trial list, we collect your email address solely to deliver the freebie and occasional product updates. We never sell personal data.

Data sharing

We do not share personal data with third parties except the service providers needed to operate the shop (payment processing via Stripe, email delivery via Formspree), each governed by their own privacy policies.
