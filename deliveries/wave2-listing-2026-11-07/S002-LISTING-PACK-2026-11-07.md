A: Print the page, write your own amounts in the blank squares or coins with a pen, then color one in each time you save. There is a blank Total line to write your own goal total.

Q: Which file do I print?
A: Print the file that fits your paper: A4 or US Letter. Both have the same 11 pages.

Q: How do I get my files?
