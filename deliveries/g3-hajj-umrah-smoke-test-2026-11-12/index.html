Hajj & Umrah Savings Planner

Save for the journey, one month at a time.

Hajj is a once-in-a-lifetime journey — and a large, long-term cost. This planner turns the big package price into a monthly savings goal, and keeps the whole trip — packages, documents, timeline and rituals — in one place.

One plan for the money and the journey.

A large goal needs a monthly number. The planner gives you the package target and the monthly saving, plus the comparison and the checklists, so saving and planning live in one place.

What it would be

  • A savings goal page — set your package cost target, and the planner works out how much to save each month.
  • A three-package comparison table — price, hotel, transport and visa, side by side.
  • A Nusuk, visa and vaccine timeline — what to do, and by when, checked against the current official requirements for your nationality and departure point.
  • Document and packing checklists — passport, vaccine certificate, permit, and what to pack.
  • A Makkah and Madinah schedule — separate Hajj and Umrah checklists, with itinerary details based on your chosen package and current official guidance.
  • An emergency contacts page.
Printable PDF (20–24 pages) + a spreadsheet for savings tracking. A4 + US Letter. Gregorian + Hijri dates. English, with Arabic ritual terms (ihram, tawaf, sa'i) kept in Arabic and explained in English.
