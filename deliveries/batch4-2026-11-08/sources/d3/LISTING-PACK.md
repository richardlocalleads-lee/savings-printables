Paid every two weeks — but your bills are monthly? That's why monthly budgets keep failing you. This planner follows your paydays instead.

The Biweekly Paycheck Budget is built around one core page: the Bill Split Planner. List every monthly bill once, assign each one to Paycheck 1 or Paycheck 2 by its due date, and no paycheck ever has to carry the whole month again. Twice a year, a month brings a third paycheck — and since your monthly bills are already split between the first two, most of that extra paycheck is not spoken for yet. There is a page to give it a job before it disappears, too.

WHAT'S INSIDE (14 printable template pages):
• Quick Start — budget by paycheck, not by month, in 5 steps
• Payday Map — your year's paydays, with the two 3-paycheck months marked
• Bill Split Planner — every bill assigned to the paycheck that pays it
• Pay Period Overview — planned vs. actual for one pay period
• Paycheck 1 Worksheet + Paycheck 2 Worksheet
• Expense Log — tagged by paycheck (P1 / P2)
• Debt Payoff — by paycheck, snowball or avalanche order
• Sinking Funds — big yearly costs split into per-paycheck amounts
• Savings Goal — one goal, one amount per paycheck, 80 progress boxes
• Bonus Paycheck Plan — for the 3rd paycheck months
