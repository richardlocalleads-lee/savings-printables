• This organizer is a living file, not a form you fill in once and lock away.

PLEASE NOTE:
• This is a digital download. No physical item will be shipped.
• You will need a PDF reader to open and print the files.

Q: How many accounts or properties can it hold?
A: As many as you have. Each template is one page per item — print one copy per bank account, one per property, one per insurance policy. A simple household might print 3 pages; a complex one might print 20.

Q: I have accounts in different places. Does it cover that?
A: Yes. The account template has room for the bank, the account name, the last four digits only, what the account is for, and who to contact. Never write full passwords or PINs in this book — the template says so on the page.

Q: Which file do I print?
A: Print the file that fits your paper: A4 or US Letter. Both have the same 15 pages.

Q: How do I get my files?
