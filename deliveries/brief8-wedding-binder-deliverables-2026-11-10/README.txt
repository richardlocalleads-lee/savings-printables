Wedding Binder - Printable Planner (34 pages)
=============================================

WHAT YOU GET
Four PDF files, one for each combination of language and paper size:
- Wedding-Binder_EN_A4.pdf      English, A4
- Wedding-Binder_EN_Letter.pdf  English, US Letter
- Wedding-Binder_TC_A4.pdf      Traditional Chinese, A4
- Wedding-Binder_TC_Letter.pdf  Traditional Chinese, US Letter
Print the one file that matches your language and paper. Every file has
the same 34 pages in the same order.

WHAT IT IS
A wedding planning binder: budget, countdown checklists, vendor
comparison and payment records, guest list and seating, notes and a
wedding-day timeline - divided into five sections with printed divider
pages.

It is the paper partner to the SettledDays wedding budget spreadsheet.
The spreadsheet does the sums; this binder keeps the plans. Figures
written in the binder do not calculate - enter the same figures in the
spreadsheet to keep the totals current.

HOW TO PRINT
- Print at 100% scale (no "fit to page") on A4 or US Letter paper.
- Print the pages you need and file them in your own binder, section by
  section, with each divider page at the front of its section.
- Pages you will fill more than once can be printed as many times as you
  need - for example the spend log (pages 13-14), the vendor contacts
  and payment log (pages 20-21) and the notes pages (pages 32-33).
- The divider pages carry a printed tab on the right edge. The five tabs
  sit at different heights, so all five stay visible when the pages are
  stacked in your binder. The tabs are printed chapter markers on the
  paper - they are not tear-off or cut-out labels.

FILLING IN ON A COMPUTER
Every page can also be filled in before printing. Open the PDF in a PDF
reader (for example Adobe Acrobat Reader), click or tap a box, type,
and save the file - your entries stay in the file when you save it.
Checkboxes can be ticked on screen as well. The contents page (page 2)
has clickable links to each section divider, and each divider title
links back to the contents page. Links work on screen and do not print.

The binder is black and white with no background graphics, so it prints
cleanly on a home printer.
