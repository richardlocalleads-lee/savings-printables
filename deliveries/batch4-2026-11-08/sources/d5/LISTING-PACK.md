## 描述全文

```
LOW INCOME MINI SAVINGS CHALLENGES
Built for real paydays, not perfect months.

Most savings challenges start with amounts that assume money is left over at the end of the month. If you live paycheck to paycheck, there usually isn't any left — so this pack flips the order: pick a small total you can actually reach, save it first on payday, and let the plan bend in the weeks when the bills pile up, instead of breaking.

START WITH A TIER, NOT A MOUNTAIN
The first page of the system is a choice: $25, $50, $100 or $250. Pick the total that feels almost too easy — small and finished beats big and abandoned. Write what the money is for (a starter emergency fund is the job most people give it).

THE THREE MECHANISMS
1. Payday first. On the payday page, you move your savings amount the day money lands, then live on what is left — plus a small optional game: save just the "tail" of your paycheck amount.
2. Bill-week step-down. Some weeks, every bill lands at once. Mark those weeks in advance on the step-down card, and those weeks get a smaller job on purpose: half amount, or spare change only.
3. Skip cards. A week you cannot save gets a skip card — the reason, the next step, done. Skipping is part of the plan, not a failure, and the restart steps bring you back with no catching up.

THE 14 PAGES (IN EACH VERSION)
Cover & how-to · Pick your tier ($25 / $50 / $100 / $250) · Payday-first page (12 paydays) · $1-a-day challenge (30 days) · Spare change challenge (30 days) · $5 bill challenge (20 bills = $100) · No-spend days calendar · Mini 52-week, capped (suggested ladder never goes above $5 a week; $182 total if you follow it exactly) · The first $100 jar (color-in) · The $500 jar (color-in) · Bill-week step-down card · Skip cards & restart page · Monthly review (with one optional line to record extra debt paid) · Make-your-own page (60 blank boxes, no currency symbols)

TWO VERSIONS, THREE SIZES
Each size file holds the same 14 pages in two versions: Part 1 is the standard version with $ amounts; Part 2 is a no-currency version — same pages, currency symbols removed, amounts left blank to write in yourself, for any currency and any income (one exception: the mini 52-week page keeps its small suggested ladder in plain numbers, with a write-your-own column beside it). Sizes: A4, US Letter, and A6 (105 × 148 mm — the cash-stuffing size). Print at 100%.
Each size file is 30 pages in total: a contents page, the 14-page standard version, a divider, and the 14-page no-currency version.

Print & write by hand — no app, no account, no subscription. The interiors are black & white and ink-friendly; print only the pages you need, and print the no-spend calendar again each month.

PLEASE NOTE
```

## FAQ（Etsy FAQ 欄逐條填）

**Q: Is this a physical book or binder?**
A: No. It is a digital download — PDF files you print yourself. Nothing is shipped. The A6 file is true A6 size (105 × 148 mm), the format cash-stuffing binders use.

**Q: Do I need an app or a spreadsheet?**
A: No. The whole pack is print & write by hand. Print at 100% (actual size), grab a pen, and color in boxes as you go.

**Q: Which paper sizes are included?**
A: All three: A4, US Letter, and A6. Each size is its own file, and each file contains both the standard version (amounts in $) and the no-currency version (symbols removed, amounts blank for you to write in). One exception: the mini 52-week page keeps a small suggested ladder in plain numbers, with a write-your-own column beside it.

**Q: I get paid every two weeks / monthly. Does the payday page still work?**
A: Yes. The payday page has 12 rows and is not tied to a calendar — you write in your own payday dates. The system is built around paydays, not months, and the pages carry no printed dates, so you can start on any payday.

**Q: What if I miss a week?**
A: Then you use a skip card (page 12) — that is what they are for. Write the reason and the next step, and pick up again at the next payday. There is no catching up, and a skipped week does not break the plan.

**Q: How is this different from your Savings Challenge Tracker?**
A: The Tracker is a set of fixed-amount challenges for when you already know you can save the printed amounts. This mini pack comes first: you choose your own starting tier ($25 and up), save payday-first, step down in bill weeks, and skip weeks formally. Most people start here to build a first emergency fund, then move to the Tracker.

**Q: Is there a version without dollar signs?**
A: Yes — Part 2 of every file. Same 14 pages with the currency symbols removed and the amounts left blank, so it works with any currency. One exception: the mini 52-week page keeps a small suggested ladder in plain numbers, with a write-your-own column beside it. Page 14 (make-your-own) has no currency symbols in either version.
