Gold Savings Calculator

How many grams does your monthly saving buy?

In many Gulf households, gold is savings — bought a little at a time, kept for years, passed down. This calculator answers one question: if you set aside the same amount every month, how many grams does it buy — and how long until your target?

This is a concept we’re testing — not a product for sale yet. If enough people want it, we’ll make it — a tracker, a calculator, or both. Join the waitlist below and we’ll email you if it launches. No price is set yet, and nothing is available to buy right now.

Enter today’s per-gram price from your jeweller, for the purity you buy (21K or 24K). The price changes daily, so the answer is only as fresh as the price you type.

Prefer a paper tracker instead? We’re also testing a Gold Savings Challenge tracker — a printable grid where you record each month’s saving and the grams it bought.
