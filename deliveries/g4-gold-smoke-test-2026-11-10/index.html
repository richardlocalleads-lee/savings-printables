Gold Savings Challenge

Save toward a gold goal, one gram at a time.

In many Gulf households, gold is savings — bought a little at a time, kept for years, passed down. This tracker is built around that habit: instead of a dollar target, you set a gram target, and save toward it month by month.

This is a concept we’re testing — not a product for sale yet. If enough people want it, we’ll make it. Join the waitlist below and we’ll email you if it launches. No price is set yet, and nothing is available to buy right now.
A goal measured in grams, not dollars. Most savings challenges count money. This one counts gold: your target is a weight (for example, 10 g of 21K gold), and every month you write down what you saved and the grams it bought, until the grams add up to your target.

What it would be

  • A gram target you set — pick your goal in grams (for example, 10 g of 21K gold) and the tracker shows how far along you are.
  • A monthly savings grid — each month, record what you saved and the grams it bought at that month’s gold price.
  • Milestones along the way — watch the grams add up toward your target, so a long goal stays visible.
  • How it works — the rules on one page: how to set the target, how to record each month, and what to do when a month gets skipped.

Printable PDF, A4 + US Letter. Print it, keep it with your gold, check off one month at a time.
