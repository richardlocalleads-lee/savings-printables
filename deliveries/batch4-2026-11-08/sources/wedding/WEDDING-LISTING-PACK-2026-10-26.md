## 四、描述全文定稿（Lee 2026-10-27 已核准；视频句 gated，见第二节）

> A one-time-purchase workbook for one wedding budget: total budget, category splits, vendor payments, and paid / pending totals in one place. You plan the wedding yourself, without a wedding planner, and this sheet keeps the money side organized: who gets paid, how much, by when, and what is still to pay.
>
> WHAT IS INSIDE
> The workbook has 8 tabs. Start Here walks you through it in order. Overview holds your total budget and wedding date, and splits the budget across 15 categories. Category Budget shows budgeted, paid, pending, and remaining per category. Vendor Payments holds one row per payment: vendor, category, item, amount, due date, and status (Paid or Pending). Payment Summary totals paid and pending. Payment Schedule counts days to each due date. Budget vs Actual compares budget with actual spend per category. Custom & Notes lets you add your own categories and a contingency reserve.
> A 24-page print companion comes with the workbook in both A4 and US Letter: checklists and paper lists. The spreadsheet calculates totals; the print pages are for paper use. The print companion is a bonus, not the main product.
> A filled example is included separately. All figures in the example are fictional and only show how to fill in the sheets. The main file is blank.
>
> HOW IT WORKS
> Click the copy link that comes with the file, then choose Make a copy. The copy goes to your own Google Drive. Using a Google Sheets online copy usually needs a Google account. No seller account is needed.
> Fill in this order: enter your total budget and wedding date on Overview, check the category shares, then add one row per payment on Vendor Payments. Only the shaded cells are inputs; everything else calculates automatically. You do not write any formulas yourself.
> Status must be Paid or Pending. Use the dropdown in the Status column. On Overview, Remaining means budget not yet committed to any payment. On Payment Summary, Remaining to Pay means budget not yet paid. The two numbers answer different questions. On Payment Schedule, Days to Due counts from today; a negative number means the due date has passed. Rows appear in the order entered on Vendor Payments. Enter rows in due-date order, or sort that tab by Due Date. Payment rows do not sort themselves.
>
