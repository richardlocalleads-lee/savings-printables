## 2. 描述全文（定，逐字）

```
Interactive Weekly Planner PDF — type and tick right in the file. One week at a time, any week, any year.

PLEASE CHECK FIRST: This is a fillable PDF, not a printed book. You type into the boxes and tick the checkboxes on your computer, then save the file — what you typed stays saved when you reopen it. It also prints cleanly if you prefer paper. It is undated: no year is printed anywhere, so you fill in the months and dates yourself.

WHAT'S INSIDE:
• 10 pages, in A4 and US Letter. Both files have the same 10 pages: Cover, How to use, Month overview, Week at a glance, Priorities, Week review, Habit tracker, Brain dump, Task inbox, and Notes.
• 339 fillable fields: 77 text boxes and 262 checkboxes. Type in any box, tick any checkbox.
• Navigation is built in: a contents list on the cover, a navigation map on page 2, and a 9-tab bar at the top of every page — tap a tab to jump to that section. 100 clickable links in all.
• Month overview: one box per month. Write the month, then the dates and plans that matter.
• Week at a glance: your week, day by day, Monday to Sunday.
• Priorities: weekly focus — your top 3 this week.
• Habit tracker: one month of daily tick-offs for up to 8 habits.
• Task inbox: catch tasks as they arrive, then decide — today, or this week.
• Undated, on purpose: start in any week of any year, and reuse the file as long as you like.

HOW IT WORKS:
• Instant download after checkout. Open the PDF in a reader that supports forms — for example Adobe Acrobat Reader — then type and tick.
• Save the file. What you typed stays in the file when you reopen it (verified on Mac in Apple Preview).
• Or print it: print at 100% actual size, and every page works on its own. The links are a screen-only feature — they do not print.
• Start any week: fill in the month and the dates yourself, and reuse the same file week after week.

PLEASE NOTE:
• This is a digital download. No physical item will be shipped.
• You will need a PDF reader that supports form filling to type and tick on screen. Basic viewers that do not support forms will show the pages but may not save what you type — use Adobe Acrobat Reader (free) or Apple Preview for the best experience.
• The links work on screen only. They do not print, and they do not change the printed pages.
• For personal use only.

QUESTIONS:
Q: Can I really type into it?
A: Yes. The boxes, lines, and checkboxes are form fields. Type in any PDF reader that supports forms, save the file, and your entries stay saved. We verified this on a Mac in Apple Preview: type, save, close, reopen — everything was still there.

Q: Is it dated?
A: No. No year is printed anywhere. You fill in the months and dates yourself, start in any week of any year, and reuse the file as long as you like.

Q: Can I print it?
A: Yes. Print at 100% actual size. Every page works on its own, and the links do not print — a printed page looks like an ordinary planner page.

Q: How do I get my files?
A: Instant download. As soon as checkout is complete, the files are waiting in your Etsy Purchases. Nothing is shipped to you.

— Settled Days
Printable planners and trackers for money, home and head — black-and-white, ink-friendly, in A4 + US Letter. Questions, or a problem with your file? Message us anytime.

This digital product was created with AI assistance in design and production, with seller-directed development and review.
The preview images show the actual planner pages you will receive.
```
