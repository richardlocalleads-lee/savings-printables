## 描述全文

```
CHRISTMAS BUDGET KIT
Decide the total first - then spend the season calmly.

Most holiday overspending doesn't happen in one big moment. It happens in twenty small ones: one more gift, one more sale, one more "it's only a few dollars." This kit gives the whole season one number - a total you choose on purpose, before you spend anything - and then helps you keep it, page by page.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Set your total holiday budget first, then divide it across gifts, food, travel, decorations, and other seasonal expenses.
2. List every gift before you shop, with a cap per person (p.3-4)
3. Write spending down as you go, so the totals on p.2 are always true (p.5)
4. At the sales, plan the buys in advance - and let the 24-hour rule catch the impulses (p.7)
5. In January, look back once: the real numbers, and three things to carry into the new year (p.9)

WHAT'S INSIDE (9 PAGES)
- Cover / Start Here - the whole system on the first page, and the one rule that runs it
- Holiday Budget at a Glance - your total, the category caps, and what is spent so far
- Gift List Planner (2 pages) - 40 people, each with a gift idea, a cap, the actual spend, and bought / wrapped tick boxes
- Holiday Spending Tracker - every purchase, written down the day it happens
- Party & Food Budget - the menu, the shopping list, and the hosting extras that quietly add up
- Black Friday & Sales List - planned sale buys only, with the 24-hour rule for everything else
- Everything Else Budget - travel, cards & postage, decorations, and giving
- January Look-Back - the season in numbers, what surprised you, and three things to carry into January

PRINT & WRITE BY HAND
This is a printable kit: print the pages and fill them in by hand. No app, no account, nothing to install. Clean black-and-white design, easy on home ink. Print at 100% (actual size), and print just the pages you need - the gift lists and the tracker are made to be reprinted.

Pairs well with our Monthly Budget Planner - carry the same calm system into the new year.

1. Christmas Budget Kit - US Letter (9 pages)
2. Christmas Budget Kit - A4 (9 pages)
3. Personal Use License - the license terms in a plain-text file

PLEASE NOTE
This is a digital download; no physical item will be shipped. For personal use only. The files are in English.

Questions? Message us anytime - happy holidays!
Settled Days
```
