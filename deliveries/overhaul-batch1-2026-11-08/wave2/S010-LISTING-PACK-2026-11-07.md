## 2. 描述全文（定，逐字）

```
Study Planner Printable — every deadline and every note, in one place, for a 12-week term.

PLEASE CHECK FIRST: This planner covers 12 weeks (Week 1–12), plus a semester overview, subject notes, and a final check. It is built for one 12-week term. If your term is longer, print extra copies of a weekly page — each week stands on its own.

WHAT'S INSIDE:
• Semester overview (pages 3–4): every due date for the whole term, in date order, with a Done checkbox on each row.
• 12 weekly plan pages (pages 5–16): each week has a deadline table on top (Day, Subject, Assignment, Due — 7 rows) and a full half-page of note lines below. Write the week's dates in yourself at the top of each page.
• Subject notes (page 17): three subject blocks, each with room for class notes, assignment details, and things to look up.
• Final check (page 18): an 8-item end-of-term checklist plus an exam countdown table.
• 18 pages, in A4 and US Letter. Both files have the same 18 pages.
• Clickable contents on page 2 — tap a section to jump straight to it on screen (links work on screen only, not on paper).
• Black-and-white design, easy on ink.

HOW IT WORKS:
• Instant download after checkout. Open the PDF in your size and print the pages you need.
• Print at 100% actual size or "Fit to page".
• Start with the semester overview: fill in every due date in your first week, then add to it whenever a new date is announced.
• Each week, fill in that week's deadlines at the top and your notes below. Print extra weekly pages whenever you need more weeks or more notes.

PLEASE NOTE:
• This is a digital download. No physical item will be shipped.
• You will need a PDF reader to open and print the files.
• This planner covers one 12-week term. It does not include a calendar, a grade tracker, or subject dividers.
• For personal use only.

QUESTIONS:
Q: Is this planner dated?
A: No. The week pages are numbered Week 1–12, and you write the dates in yourself — so it works for a term starting in January or in August.

Q: What if my term is longer than 12 weeks?
A: Print extra copies of a weekly page. Each week is a self-contained page, so you can add as many weeks as you need.

Q: Which file do I print?
A: Print the file that fits your paper: A4 or US Letter. Both have the same 18 pages.

Q: How do I get my files?
A: Instant download. As soon as checkout is complete, the files are waiting in your Etsy Purchases. Nothing is shipped to you.

— Settled Days
Printable planners and trackers for money, home and head — black-and-white, ink-friendly, in A4 + US Letter. Questions, or a problem with your file? Message us anytime.
```
